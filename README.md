## Devlog


The Develog: From my personal point of view, the scences are a kind of container to contain all Gameobkects. For the Gameobjects, it refer to the various entities inside the scences, which have not function for themselves. The Components are the functional components attached to a GameObject. Thus we can seem scences like a small town, and Gameobjects are every houses, trees and cars, inside the town. In this case, components are doors, windows, pipes, and light fixtures cannot exist as a house on their own; they must be installed within the structure of a house.

In my project, I directly copy and paste MetalSpikes, coins, platforms blocks and move them to finish all the work.
